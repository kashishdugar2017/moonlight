Sevoflurane is a medicine. Doctors already use it for something else. It grabs a protein called KCNK3. A protein is a tiny machine in your body. Sevoflurane turns KCNK3 up, like a volume knob. KCNK3 is linked to pulmonary arterial hypertension. That is high blood pressure in the arteries of the lungs. An artery is like a pipe for blood. The link score is 0.79. That is like 79 points out of 100. Sevoflurane has no link to this disease in the database. The database is a huge web with lots of information.
